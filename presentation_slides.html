Problem Statement

The Challenge: Fragmented CCTV & Intelligence Gaps

1. Siloed Surveillance Systems
  • CCTV cameras deployed by Police, RTO, Municipalities, and Mining operate in complete isolation.
  • No centralized metadata catalog to trace camera ownership, stream URLs, or operational status.
  • Causes heavy administrative delays during emergency multi-district investigations.
2. Spatial Blind Spots & Gap Blindness
  • Without GIS spatial indexing, security forces cannot visualize camera coverage radii.
  • Highways and critical transit corridors suffer from undetected surveillance gaps.
  • Inability to plan future camera deployments based on empirical spatial coverage data.
3. Delayed Suspect Intercepts
  • Manual video retrieval from local NVRs delays suspect tracking by hours or days.
  • No automated chronological route reconstruction across sequential highway camera nodes.
  • Lack of real-time control room push notifications when a hotlist vehicle triggers an ANPR match.
Solution Overview

SentinelGrid: The Unified Intelligence Grid

🌐 State-Wide GIS Camera Registry
  • Standardized camera catalog with PostGIS GEOGRAPHY(POINT,4326) coordinates.
  • Manual single-camera onboarding form + CSV bulk upload with pre-commit validation.
  • Live camera status indicators: Active, Offline, Maintenance, Legacy, and Tagged Live Test nodes.
🗺️ PostGIS Spatial Gap Analysis
  • Executes ST_DWithin spatial queries to calculate coverage radiuses and highlight uncovered zones.
  • Empowers law enforcement to strategically place new CCTV nodes along unmonitored highway stretches.
  • Department-scoped spatial layer toggling (Police, RTO, Mining, Municipal).
⚡ AI Watchlist & Real-Time Alert Ticker
  • 5 Watchlist Categories: Stolen Vehicle, Wanted Person, Missing Person, Blacklisted Vehicle, Suspect.
  • ANPR license plate extraction & facial feature cosine similarity matching engine.
  • FastAPI WebSockets push sub-100ms alerts with audio chime and pulsing Leaflet map pins.
🛣️ Chronological Vehicle Route Tracking
  • Step 4 Centerpiece: Renders connected route polyline across sequential camera nodes.
  • Animated map marker playback controls (Play, Pause, Speed multiplier).
  • Interactive timeline sidebar with speed telemetry (74.2 km/h avg) and OCR confidence metrics.
Architecture & Rationale

System Architecture & Hybrid Model (Model 1 + Model 3)

Hybrid Operating Model Justification
  • Model 1 (CCTV Registry & GIS): Establishes camera spatial ownership, stream protocols, and coverage gap detection.
  • Model 3 (Watchlist Analytics): Executes ANPR plate reading and facial recognition against active hotlists.
  • Why SentinelGrid Hybrid Wins: Model 1 alone is passive metadata; Model 3 alone lacks GIS route context. Combining them provides real-time AI alerts over a true spatial GIS topology.
Tech Stack Engineering Rationale
  • Frontend: React.js + Vite + Leaflet.js (Fast glassmorphism UI, lightweight vector tiles).
  • Backend Engine: Python + FastAPI (Async WebSockets, native OpenCV/ONNX ML pipeline integration).
  • Spatial DB: PostgreSQL 15 + PostGIS 3.3 (Spherical spatial geography indexing).
  • Orchestration: Docker Compose (Single-command container deployment).
PostGIS Engine

PostGIS Spatial Indexing & Gap Detection

PostGIS Technical Implementation
GEOGRAPHY(POINT, 4326)

  • Spatial Query Example:
  • SELECT * FROM cameras WHERE ST_DWithin(location, ST_MakePoint(:lng, :lat)::geography, :radius_meters);
  • Guarantees accurate distance calculations over spherical Earth geometry.
  • Scales efficiently across 100,000+ camera nodes with PostGIS GIST spatial indices.
Uncovered Zone Overlay & Infrastructure Flags
  • Visualizes 500m/1km buffer circles around camera points to pinpoint surveillance dark zones.
  • Supports department-based layer filtering (Police vs RTO vs Mining).
  • Legacy Infrastructure Flag: Highlights aging cameras needing hardware upgrades.
  • Tagged Live Test Flag: Identifies newly onboarded cameras in trial mode.

AI Watchlist Engine

AI Matching Pipeline & Human-in-the-Loop Policy

5 Named Watchlist Categories
  • stolen_vehicle — Automated ANPR license plate matching.
  • wanted_person — Facial embedding vector cosine similarity matching.
  • missing_person — High-priority civilian search hotlist.
  • blacklisted_vehicle — RTO default / tax evasion watchlist.
  • suspect — Active law enforcement surveillance targets.
False-Positive Mitigation & HITL Policy
  • 85.0% Confidence Cutoff: Match confidence below 85.0% triggers an automated secondary visual check.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Mandate: No automated pursuit is dispatched solely by AI. Control room officers MUST visually confirm license plate and vehicle image before dispatching units.
  • Drastically reduces false-positive field deployments.

Sub-100ms Real-Time WebSocket Alert Engine

WebSocket Architecture
  • FastAPI native async WebSocket Connection Manager broadcasts alert payloads instantly.
  • Sub-100ms latency from initial ANPR camera trigger to control room browser UI render.

Control Room UX & Audio-Visual Feedback

Scalability & Resiliency

Enterprise Scalability & Disaster Recovery (DR)

Scalability Architecture
  • Stateless FastAPI Backend: Scales horizontally behind NGINX load balancer.
  • PostgreSQL Partitioning: Spatial index partitioning by district and timestamp.
  • PgBouncer Connection Pooling: Manages thousands of simultaneous database connections efficiently.
Disaster Recovery (DR) & VMS Adapters
  • Ingestion Adapters: Normalizes local RTSP feeds and Govt Central CCTV REST streams into standardized JSON event frames.
  • DR Failover Simulation: Built-in UI toggle demonstrates seamless failover from Primary Central Server to Secondary Standby Node.
  • Zero Single Point of Failure (SPOF) design.
Data Governance

DPDP Act 2023 Compliance & Privacy Framing

DPDP Act 2023 Compliance
  • Purpose Limitation: Metadata processing strictly restricted to public safety mandates.
  • Non-Watchlist Data Retention: Video frames and non-matching ANPR reads are purged automatically after 30 days.
  • Privacy Masking: Automated blurring of bystander faces during evidence export.
Security & Cryptographic Admissibility
  • TLS 1.3 & AES-256: Full encryption in transit and at rest.
  • SHA-256 Hashing: Event snapshot evidence is cryptographically signed to guarantee court admissibility.
  • Prevents unauthorized evidence tampering or data leaks.
